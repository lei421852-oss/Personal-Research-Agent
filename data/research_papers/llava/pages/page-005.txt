Stage 1: Pre-training for Feature Alignment. To strike a balance between concept coverage and training efﬁciency, we ﬁlter CC3M to 595K image-text pairs. Each sample can be treated as a single-turn conversation. To construct the input Xinstruct in (2), for an image Xv, a question Xq in Table 8 is randomly sampled, which is a language instruction to request the assistant to describe the image brieﬂy. The prediction answer Xa is the original caption. In training, we keep both the visual encoder and LLM weights frozen, and maximize the likelihood of (3) with trainable parameters θ = W (the projection matrix) only. In this way, the image features Hv can be aligned with the pre-trained LLM word embedding. This stage can be understood as training a compatible visual tokenizer for the frozen LLM.
